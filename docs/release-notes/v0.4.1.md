This patch restores missing inventory categories in Bag.

## Fixes

- Materials, consumables, and cosmetics now appear in both Bag and Storage, including their category sections, quantities, search results, and inspector details. Previously these categories were enabled only for Storage.
- These categories remain excluded from market estimates until pricing support is available, preventing unrelated listings from producing misleading totals.

After updating, switch maps to capture your bag again, and open or interact with personal storage to capture its contents.

## Updating

Installed users can choose Settings > Check for updates, then Update and restart. Windows users downloading manually should choose `ValeCompanion-0.4.1-windows-x64-setup.exe`. Portable users should replace the executable while preserving their data folder.

Windows binaries are unsigned and require Npcap. Linux packages include AppImage, DEB, and RPM. Matching source, updater metadata, blockmaps, and SHA256SUMS.txt are included.

## Validation

109 tests, typechecking, and the local build passed. The fix was verified locally by the user. Windows and Linux package smoke tests and updater-file checks run before publication.
